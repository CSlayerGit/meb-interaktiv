5c–dFaltungsintegral: y(t) = ∫ x(τ) · g(t − τ) dτ

The impulse response is g(t) = ḣ(t) = 2e−tσ(t). Flip it and move it to position t: g(t − τ) starts at τ = t and decays to the left. y(t) is the shaded area under x(τ)·g(t − τ). The two σ factors set the integration limits.
